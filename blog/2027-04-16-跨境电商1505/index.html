The AliExpress Spring Sale opens today

The structural fact on this Thursday is that the AliExpress Spring Sale opens today, April 16, and runs through April 22. The coupon pre-collect window that peaked yesterday at T-minus-one is now live: the discounts are loaded, the stacks are active, and the cart you built yesterday transacts at the open. The April 8 to 15 natural-fiber and sustainable-goods window closed at the April 15 reset last night. The event floor is gone, the colourways and sizes that were scarce yesterday are now gone, and the live-variant run has emptied. For a buyer who missed the early-bird floor, the instruction is no longer urgent but deliberate: the natural-fiber demand is still here (it is still April, still spring), so the Earth Day purchase now shifts to the Spring Sale, which is broader but at standard event pricing rather than the early-bird floor. Complete the natural-fiber or recycled purchase through the Spring Sale stack, not the post-reset sticker, because the early-bird cheap buy is no longer on the shelf.

The post-Easter clearance is done

The post-Easter clearance that drained to zero by the April 15 reset is finished. The refurb and open-box channels that showed final markdown yesterday are now empty, and the deepest single-item discounts of the spring are gone. There is no more clearance depth to chase. The buyer read is that the calendar has fully moved from clearance to event: the Spring Sale is the only live discount engine now, and the commodity-goods hold call from earlier in the week has resolved in the Sale’s favour - the one-day wait to April 16 was worth it for fungible goods.

2. News and Market Movements on 2027-04-16

The news flow this Thursday is the opening bell of the spring-event stack. The AliExpress Spring Sale open is the loudest signal, with coupon stacks going live and smart-home outdoor gear and garden hardware surfacing as the first-day leaders. The Earth Day reset is the second signal, with natural-fiber sellers reporting end-of-window liquidation completes and a clean close as the assortment empties. The post-Easter clearance close is the third, with refurb channels confirming zero-stock drain at final markdown.

The structural backdrop this spring is the one that has defined 2027: the de minimis world is fully closed on both sides of the Atlantic and is about to be codified into permanent law. The US $800 exemption has been suspended since August 29, 2025 and confirmed indefinite; the EU EUR 150 exemption ended July 1, 2026 with a EUR 3 per-item flat duty; and the Section 321 commercial-shipment repeal takes effect July 1, 2027, less than three months from today. That means this spring is the first full selling season where every low-value parcel carries a duty obligation by default, so the durable buyer test is unchanged and sharper: the total delivered cost must be visible before the click, and the seller who holds stock in the buyer region is the one who survives the next policy step.

4. Independent-Site Dynamics on 2027-04-16

Shopify DTC: the garden and natural-fiber advantage compounds into the Sale

Independent Shopify stores are not subject to the marketplace repricing churn, and for freight-hostile and seasonal-garden categories they hold a structural edge: the item already sits in a regional warehouse, so the checkout price is the delivered price. As the spring parcel environment keeps the marketplace alternative tight and the Earth Day reset empties the marketplace natural-fiber assortment, that edge compounds for ceramics, heavy wood, stoneware and garden hardware. The stores worth watching are the ones that disclose landed cost at checkout and hold stock in the buyer region, because they are the only ones still reliably in stock after the early-bird window closes.

The disclosed-landed-cost test, now the law of the land by July

The durable test for an independent store is whether the delivered total is visible before the click. With the Section 321 commercial repeal landing July 1, 2027, stores that show the landed cost at checkout are pricing honestly under the closed-de-minimis environment. Stores that hide shipping until late in the flow are the ones most exposed to a correction. The buyer move is to filter on the disclosed total, not the sticker, and to favour the store that will still be trading after the next policy change.
