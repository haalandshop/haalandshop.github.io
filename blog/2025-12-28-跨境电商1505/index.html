Cross-Border E-Commerce Daily: The Post-Christmas Clearance Stack and Pre-Resolution Basket

Daily market read for 2025-12-28 - platform dynamics, hot sectors, and 20 products worth buying across marketplaces and independent DTC stores.

December 28 2025 is the Sunday of the post-Christmas dead-zone-that-isn’t - the third day of the after-Christmas clearance window and three days before New Year’s Eve. The demand signal that matters most today is not a tariff or a logistics cutoff (those have already closed for the season) but the collision of three forces: post-holiday clearance repricing, the opening of the New-Year-resolution buy cycle, and the last clean restocking window before the Chinese New Year factory shutdown that will freeze cross-border supply for weeks in early 2026. Below is the full read on platform dynamics, the day’s news, hot sectors, and 20 products worth buying across marketplaces and independent DTC stores.

1. Major Platform Product Dynamics on December 28 2025

Amazon (North America / EU / Japan). After-Christmas clearance is live and the cleanest movers are resolution and winter SKUs that are already US- or EU-warehoused. Fitness trackers, smart scales, air fryers and heated throws screen well because they ride the resolution wave and ship from domestic stock, so they dodge the de-minimis uplift and the season’s closed ocean windows. Amazon US is also absorbing the post-holiday return flow, which keeps FBA storage tight and pushes sellers toward fast-moving, high-turn fungibles rather than slow clearance deadweight.

TikTok Shop (US / UK / SEA). Content-commerce keeps compounding into year-end. New-Year-resolution and NYE-party content (fitness demos, cozy-home setups, karaoke and fairy-light room transforms) is the dominant short-video theme, and the categories that demo best on camera - wellness wearables, LED and smart-home cozy gear, party audio - are the named conversion leaders. Live sessions lean into new-year-new-me hauls, and merchant self-content plus creator collabs carry the volume.

AliExpress / Shopee / Lazada. The SEA trio is in year-end clearance after the December 12 super-sale closed earlier in the month. AliExpress trends to ultra-low-price resolution gadgets and winter warmers; Shopee and Lazada are loading clearance vouchers on smart-home, LED and small appliances. The formula is clearance voucher plus price edge, with Live still driving the tail of December GMV. Lazada’s app-controlled winter and connected-living fungibles are the quiet SEA winner riding the same wave.

SHEIN / Temu. Both keep converting from cross-border direct shipping to localized inventory to dodge per-parcel duties, and the post-Christmas window is their clearance lever. SHEIN’s US semi-managed fulfillment compresses delivery and its cozy loungewear and resolution-active lines are the apparel read. Temu requires overseas-warehouse cover of at least 14 days of sales and throttles sellers under stock-health thresholds, so its impulse winter and resolution lines only ship fast if pre-positioned.

Walmart / Target / eBay. Walmart and Target are the Western physical-trade anchors for after-Christmas clearance and winter small appliances; the compact air fryer and heated throw are the clean reads. eBay leans into resolution-adjacent resale - fitness gear, audio, smart-home - where resale value rose after the de-minimis change, making it a quietly strong vertical for value buyers.

2. Cross-Border Product Trends and News (December 28 2025)

Five signals move product on December 28, and the common thread is clearance-plus-resolution.

The after-Christmas clearance window is three days in. Amazon, Walmart and Target are repricing winter and resolution fungibles; voucher math on smart-home, LED and small appliances clears hardest in the December 26 to 31 window, so this week is the last clean stacking opportunity before the New Year.

The New-Year-resolution buy cycle is opening. Fitness trackers, smart scales, water bottles, planners and air fryers lift ahead of January; the buy signal is the resolution theme, not discount depth.

The NYE-party micro-window is three days out. Karaoke mics, fairy lights, LED strips and party audio convert on TikTok Live and short video; this is the last demoable party buy before the calendar turns.

The CNY restocking urgency is real. With the Chinese New Year factory shutdown approaching in early 2026, cross-border sellers are locking in-region inventory now; SKUs already warehoused in the US or EU defend speed while direct-from-China lines risk weeks of blank supply.

The landed-cost floor is permanent. The de-minimis repeal and the EU flat-fee import structure are settled; per-parcel duty and handling are now baked into cross-border math, so the worth-buying edge is timing and geography, not waiting for a lower landed cost that will not return.

3. Hot Sectors and Category Momentum (Marketplaces)

Resolution wearables lead. Fitness trackers and smart scales are the category’s annual demand opener; demoable, giftable, and US-warehoused units convert without freight risk.

Cozy winter is the quiet compounder. Heated throws and weighted blankets ride the post-holiday stay-in mood and the gifting tail; they are bulky to ship from China, so in-region stock is the differentiator.

Smart-home fungibles are voucher-driven. LED strips and WiFi bulbs clear hardest on SEA year-end voucher stacks and demo in seconds on video, making them the cleanest content-commerce fungibles of the week.

Small appliances hold the clearance anchor. The compact air fryer is the resolution cooking hero and the Western physical-trade clearance read; it is demoable in fifteen seconds and already US-stocked.

NYE party is the timed spike. Karaoke mics and fairy lights are a three-day conversion window pinned to December 31; buy the specific variant now or skip the window.

Winter charging and hydration round out the basket. GaN chargers and power banks (travel and creator charging) plus smart water bottles complete the resolution-and-cozy stack that feeds every Live session.

4. Independent Site (DTC) Dynamics and Hot Sectors

Shopify DTC stores are running the same clearance-plus-resolution playbook without the marketplace tournament. The read that matters: DTC winners are US-warehoused, affiliate-ready, and built to lift average order value through bundles.

The resolution and cozy stack is the DTC backbone - wellness wearables (Oura, Whoop), connected-living (Ember smart mugs, Loftie clocks, HidrateSpark bottles), and cozy sleep (Bearaby weighted blankets) - all demoable, all content-worthy, all strong gifting.

The connected-gifting edge is uniquely DTC. Aura digital frames and similar decor-tech convert as premium gifts that marketplaces under-merchandise; the maker controls the story and the bundle.

The travel and fitness-apparel tail (Away luggage, Gymshark tees) rides New-Year travel and the resolution gym restart; both are US-warehoused and creator-collab ready, so they hold speed and content pull into January.

The standing DTC caveat holds: heavy or custom items still carry return freight on the buyer, so the worth-buying call favors in-region, fungible, bundle-friendly SKUs over bulky imports.

Anker and Baseus winter chargers and power banks (travel and creator charging, US-warehoused), Amazfit and Fitbit fitness trackers (resolution, demoable, giftable), and heated throws (FBA fast delivery) are the three cleanest late-December reads; all dodge the de-minimis uplift and convert on video.
  • Shopify. Oura and Whoop wellness wearables, Ember smart mugs, Loftie alarm clocks and HidrateSpark bottles form the resolution and cozy stack; US-warehoused, affiliate-ready, high AOV through bundles.
  • TikTok Shop. Fitness trackers and karaoke mics (short-video demoable, high AOV, FBT-fulfilled) plus RGB strip lights and fairy lights riding the NYE and cozy wave are the breakout best-sellers for the run into January.

Discount depth on event merchandise is real but funded by volume; non-event promotional depth compresses. SKUs already warehoused in-region defend speed; anything relying on a fresh China shipment risks blank listings. Front-run the freeze by buying specific in-region winter and resolution items before late January.

Weight every buy toward items already warehoused in-region, favor demoable fungibles during voucher windows, and front-run the CNY supply freeze by buying specific resolution and cozy items before late January.
