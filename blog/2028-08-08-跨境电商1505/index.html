Executive Summary

The cross-border market on 2028-08-08 is defined by one word: live. In parallel, back-to-school demand closes its final shopping week in the US and EU ahead of late-August move-in, so dorm tech and study gear still convert on peak intent but the window is now shutting. And late-summer clearance deepens as patio, outdoor and summer apparel take their steepest markdowns of the season ahead of the fall reset and the September 4 Labor Day weekend.

Three things frame the date.

First, the 8.8 mega-sale floor is live. The tactical edge that existed yesterday (pre-load a wishlist, fire at the open) is gone; today the deals are real but competitive, so the buyer wins by redeeming stacked vouchers and free-shipping coupons on wireless audio, smart-home accessories, small kitchen appliances and beauty bundles rather than by chasing scattered pre-heat markdowns.

Laptop stands, noise-cancel headphones, desk lamps, mini fridges and linen bedding convert on last-call urgency across Amazon and TikTok Shop, but the category rotates toward clearance and restock as campuses reopen, so the best prices are now or never for this cycle.

Third, late-summer clearance deepens. Grills, patio furniture, camping gear and summer fashion hit their lowest prices of the season from US and EU local stock with zero duty exposure, so the value lane is real for patient, non-urgent buyers.

The through-line is redeem the 8.8 stack, clear back-to-school at last call, and cherry-pick the deepening late-summer clearance.
