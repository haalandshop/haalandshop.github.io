Cross-Border E-Commerce Pulse: The Last Dispatch Before the Lunar Shutdown

Monday, 28 January 2027 sits nine days before the Lunar New Year 2027 factory shutdown (Saturday, 6 February, Year of the Goat). Ocean cut-off for the holiday has already passed for most lanes. What still moves is air freight and, more important, inventory that sellers pre-positioned in Amazon FBA, third-party overseas warehouses, and Shopify 3PLs before the closure. Valentine’s Day and Super Bowl LXI both land on 14 February 2027, so the demand window for gifting and game-day goods is locked: anything not already in-region must ship by air or it misses the peak. The macro backdrop is the post-de-minimis world. The US $800 exemption has been gone since 29 August 2025, every inbound parcel now carries duty plus a per-item processing fee, and the EU killed its 150-euro threshold on 1 July 2026 with a 3-euro-per-sub-heading Union fee layered on from November 2026. That environment rewards sellers who hold local stock and punishes pure direct-from-China dropshipping. Below is the daily read on where the heat is, and what is worth buying right now.

1. Executive Pulse

The cross-border market keeps growing even as margins compress. Global cross-border GMV is tracking toward roughly $6.5 trillion in 2026, up about 12 percent year over year, with apparel near 40 percent of spend, consumer electronics around 20 percent, and health and beauty in double-digit growth. The split that matters for buyers today is stock location. Items sitting in US and EU fulfillment centers convert and ship on time. Items still in a Shenzhen warehouse on 28 January will not reach a Western doorstep before Valentine’s Day without air, and air is expensive after the de-minimis removal. So the practical buyer’s rule for this date: favor FBA and warehouse-stocked SKUs, treat Temu and AliExpress as restock-only for non-urgent goods, and lean on TikTok Shop and DTC for trend and gifting.

2. Platform Momentum

Amazon remains the default Everything Store with roughly $362 billion in GMV across domains. The 2027 story is AI-assisted shopping (Alexa for Shopping) pulling demand inward and a clear tiering where sellers doing over $1 million a year now exceed 51,000. For buyers, Amazon is the safest bet for in-stock electronics, home, and beauty at predictable speed.

TikTok Shop is the growth engine. Global GMV ran past $54 billion in 2026 and Q1 2026 hit $27.45 billion, up 95 percent, with the US quarter near $6.99 billion. About 51 percent of TikTok Shop sellers reported rising revenue, the highest of any platform. The catch is concentration: the top 1 percent of sellers drive roughly 60 percent of GMV, so viral items sell out fast. Beauty, personal care, and snackable gadgets lead.

AliExpress still dominates China-origin B2C volume and has added tariff estimates at checkout, which spiked cart abandonment but improved transparency. Useful for price comparison and non-urgent restock, weaker for time-critical gifting.

Temu shifted from full-managed to semi-managed and holds around 120 million US monthly users, but US orders fell 35 to 50 percent after the de-minimis end as it moved to US warehousing. Prices are still low on warehouse-stocked goods; direct shipping is slower and taxed.

Shopee and Lazada hold over 60 percent of Indonesia and Vietnam GMV. TikTok Shop actually overtook Shopee in Vietnam in Q1 2026, signaling a Southeast Asia content-commerce flip. For Western buyers these are less relevant, but they set the global price floor that Temu and SHEIN benchmark against.

Walmart and eBay are the domestic-fulfillment winners of the new tariff era. Walmart accelerated nearshoring, and eBay’s July 2026 commission restructure (electronics up to 7 percent, home and sport up to 14 percent) squeezed small sellers but kept refurb and second-hand liquidation deals attractive.

4. Independent Site (DTC / Shopify) Momentum

DTC brands that hold US and EU inventory are the quiet winners of the post-de-minimis reset. They never relied on the $800 loophole, so their landed cost is stable while marketplace dropshippers reprice upward. The 2027 DTC heat centers on three themes: wellness subscriptions (vitamins, sleep, oral care), premium home (cookware, stoneware, bedding), and travel and apparel built for the Valentine and resort window. Shopify-native brands with 3PL stock can promise 2 to 4 day delivery through the holiday, which marketplaces dependent on air cannot match at scale. Expect DTC to keep taking share from Temu and AliExpress on anything time-sensitive.

8. 30 to 90 Day Outlook

The next quarter splits into two phases. From 28 January to 6 February, supply is frozen as Chinese factories close for the Goat-year holiday; only air and in-region stock move, so prices on warehouse-light SKUs tick up. From mid-February through April, restocked ocean freight arrives, Temu and AliExpress relist delayed SKUs, and the post-holiday resolution and spring-cleaning wave lifts home, fitness, and organization categories. TikTok Shop should keep outpacing on beauty and personal care as the platform’s AI creative studio scales seller content. Amazon holds on electronics and home. DTC brands with 3PL depth widen their speed advantage. The de-minimis and EU fee structure is settled for now, so the winners are operators who localized inventory before the shutdown rather than those racing air freight at the wire.

9. Compliance and Logistics Watch

Three items shape every buy decision this week. First, the US de-minimis $800 exemption is gone for all countries since 29 August 2025; every parcel faces duty plus a per-item processing fee, so direct-from-China carts cost more and arrive later. Second, the EU abolished its 150-euro duty threshold on 1 July 2026 and added a 3-euro-per-sub-heading Union fee from November 2026, pushing EU buyers toward locally held stock. Third, the UK 135-pound low-value rule rolls into 2027 with the same local-stock logic. Practical implication: for anything needed by 14 February 2027, buy FBA, Walmart, eBay, or DTC 3PL stock; treat Temu, AliExpress, and SHEIN as restock-only unless the listing shows in-region warehouse inventory.

10. Closing

The 28 January 2027 read is a supply-and-timing story more than a demand story. Demand is clear: Valentine gifting, Super Bowl hosting, resolution fitness, and Lunar New Year travel. Supply is the constraint, and it is frozen in nine days. Buyers who move on warehouse-stocked marketplace and DTC items today still catch the peak; everyone else is betting on air freight or waiting for the February restock. Favor local stock, watch the beauty and electronics heat on TikTok Shop and Amazon, and keep Temu and AliExpress in the cart for non-urgent 2027 restock.
