2. News and Market Movements on 2027-04-15

The news flow this Wednesday is the closing bell of the spring-event stack. The Earth Day early-bird peak is the loudest and final signal, with natural-fiber and recycled-goods sellers reporting end-of-window liquidation traffic as buyers chase the last hours before the April 15 reset. The post-Easter clearance is the second signal, with refurb and open-box channels reporting genuine zero-stock drain at final markdown. The AliExpress Spring Sale pre-heat is the third, with coupon-collection counts peaking one day ahead of the open and smart-home outdoor gear surfacing as the pre-heat leader.

The structural backdrop this spring is the one that has defined 2027: the de minimis world is fully closed on both sides of the Atlantic and is about to be codified into permanent law. The US $800 exemption has been suspended since August 29, 2025 and confirmed indefinite; the EU €150 exemption ended July 1, 2026 with a €3 per-item flat duty; and the Section 321 commercial-shipment repeal takes effect July 1, 2027, less than three months from today. That means this spring is the first full selling season where every low-value parcel carries a duty obligation by default, so the durable buyer test is unchanged and sharper: the total delivered cost must be visible before the click, and the seller who holds stock in the buyer region is the one who survives the next policy step.

Independent Shopify stores are not subject to the marketplace repricing churn, and for freight-hostile and seasonal-garden categories they hold a structural edge: the item already sits in a regional warehouse, so the checkout price is the delivered price.
