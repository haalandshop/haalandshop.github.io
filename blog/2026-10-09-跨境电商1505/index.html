Forward Outlook: Worth-Buying Trajectory Past 10.10

The T-1 Eve Price-Freezecountdown

From October 9 the clock to the 10.10 open is exactly 24 hours. The 11.11 Pre-Collect Lock Watch

The lock watch is category-specific. Fungible volume items - pet mats, cutlery sets, cables, linens - almost always net cheaper at 10.10 once payday and event vouchers stack, and the 11.11 pre-collect floor rarely beats the combined 10.10 discount plus shipping. Specific variants, single-run goods and high-ticket items still favour 11.11 depth. The rule: apply the lock watch only to purchases you have already decided on, and let the fungible-versus-specific split drive the call.

Buyer Risk and Compliance Notes

Three risks sit under every buy on October 9. The first is the uneven restock fill: assume thinner fungible EU and small-appliance stock after Golden Week and do not commit to post-wall inbound without regional stock. Compliance discipline for the buyer: verify CE or FCC where the category requires it, confirm the EU 150 EUR relief is already abolished so per-sub-heading 3 EUR handling applies to EU-bound goods, and treat the UK 135 GBP threshold as still in consultation rather than a live relief.
