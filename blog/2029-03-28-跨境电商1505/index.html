1. Platform Dynamics on 2029-03-28

The spring peak holds for one more day before the restage begins

The structural fact on this Wednesday is that the spring selling season is still at peak depth, but the clock has visibly moved. The AliExpress Spring Sale is in its final core-run days, the Northern Hemisphere planting and home-refresh window is fully open, and the pre-Easter build (Easter lands April 21, 2029) is now accelerating rather than merely visible. Because demand peaks roughly three to five weeks ahead of the holiday, March 28 is the day the peak week still has live event depth but the repricing teams have already started modelling the late-April step-up. The buyer move is to transact today, not tomorrow: the variant gaps that widen through the restage begin exactly here, at the back end of the peak.

The five-tier duty structure is now settled normal, not transition

The EU five-tier duty schedule, live since July 1, 2028, is no longer a novelty but the permanent operating backdrop, and that changes how the board reads. The flat de-minimis world is gone and replaced by a settled, multi-rate regime where landed cost is tiered by product category and declared value. For a buyer that means the disclosed-landed-cost line at checkout is the decisive signal between two otherwise identical listings, because the duty tier can swing the delivered total by more than the event discount. The stores that survived the first full cycle are the ones that priced honestly under the tiers; the ones that did not have thinned out, and the restage will not bring them back.

Southeast Asia keeps rolling into the mid-year ramp

Shopee and Lazada remain on the mid-year ramp rather than a spring-event lane, with voucher and coin stacks building toward the 6.6 and 7.7 windows. For SEA buyers the March 28 window is still the early accumulation phase: collect vouchers now, transact at the mid-year peak. The compliance and landed-cost test is identical to the West, but the event clock points toward summer, not Easter, so the urgency to transact today is lower in SEA than in the Northern Hemisphere spring markets.

2. News and Market Movements on 2029-03-28

The news flow this Wednesday is the spring peak holding for one more day while the pre-Easter build accelerates, sitting on top of the fully matured five-tier duty regime. The AliExpress Spring Sale core run is the loudest signal, with garden hardware, natural-fiber home goods and smart-home outdoor gear still holding the lead as the final core-run days tick down. The pre-Easter build is the second signal, with giftable home and garden items climbing faster now that the holiday is within five weeks. The freight-and-policy backdrop is the third, and it is the settled normal rather than a transition.

The structural backdrop for late March 2029 is the one the whole cycle has been moving toward: the de minimis floor is gone and the replacement is mature. The US $800 exemption was suspended August 29, 2025 and confirmed indefinite; the EU EUR 150 exemption ended July 1, 2026 with a EUR 3 per-item flat duty; the OBBBA statutory repeal of Section 321 de minimis took effect July 1, 2027; and the EU five-tier duty structure has been live since July 1, 2028, now past its first full cycle. That means this spring is the second full selling season under permanent de-minimis repeal and the first spring entirely inside the five-tier regime. Every low-value parcel carries a tiered duty obligation by default, so the durable buyer test is unchanged and sharper: the total delivered cost must be visible before the click, and the seller who holds stock in the buyer region is the one who still clears the lane.
