5. Independent-Site (DTC) Movements and Sectors

DTC momentum rides the same summer peaks but on different logic: brand trust beats marketplace price on health, wellness and recovery, and the drop model lets Gymshark-class and Beis-class stores capture margin the marketplaces cannot. Smart rings, recovery devices and sustainable travel gear are the DTC sweet spot, where direct warranty, subscription and bundle economics beat a marketplace coupon. With the post-event baseline pushing value-seeking buyers toward trusted brands, expect a mid-July lift in last-minute-summer-gifts and dorm search traffic on DTC as the marketplace base settles and the early-holiday gifting wave begins to stir.
