4.2 Why DTC and Marketplace Belong on Opposite Sides

The two tables are deliberately structured as opposites because the buyer’s job is different on each side, and pretending otherwise produces recommendations that are wrong in exactly the way that costs the most money once the deal calendar goes live and the brand map is finished. The correct mental model is two separate clocks: the platform clock and the brand clock, ticking at different speeds and striking at different moments.
