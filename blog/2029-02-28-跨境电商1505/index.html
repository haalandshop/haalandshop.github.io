Cross-Border E-Commerce Pulse: The Post-CNY Restock, Late-Winter Clearance and the Q1 Close - February 28 2029

Daily market read for 2029-02-28 - platform dynamics, hot sectors, and 20 products worth buying across marketplaces and independent DTC stores.

February 28 2029 is the last day of Q1 and the back-end of the post-Chinese-New-Year restock. The Year of the Snake New Year landed on February 13, so mainland factories, freight forwarders and Canton-corridor suppliers are fully back online this week, which means cross-border supply has normalized and the Q2 inventory-ordering window is open at pre-spring prices. It is also the floor of the late-winter clearance: heaters, electric blankets and winter apparel are at their cheapest before spring lines take the shelf. And underneath both sits the New-Year health curve - fitness, recovery and sleep tech that peaked in January and still runs hot through February as resolution demand converts to durable purchases.

For a cross-border buyer the arithmetic flips this week. After CNY, factory quotes and ocean-freight capacity are stable, so durable and gifting categories can be locked at Q1 base prices before the spring ramp reprices them. Late-winter clearance items, by contrast, are at their lowest and will disappear by mid-March, so the buy-now window is literally the next two weeks. The arbitrage is to restock durables at the post-CNY base and to sweep winter clearance before it vanishes.

This read covers platform heat, the hottest sectors, the likely Amazon / TikTok Shop / Shopify breakouts, and 20 products worth buying now - 10 marketplace and 10 independent-site - each with its platform and a live product entry link.

1. Executive Summary - Restock Opens, Clearance Closes

The structural fact about February 28 is the two curves moving in opposite directions. The post-CNY restock curve opens the Q2 inventory window at soft base prices, while the late-winter clearance curve closes at its floor. The health curve stays elevated. The smart money restocks durables and spring-adjacent SKUs this week and sweeps winter clearance before mid-March.

2. Marketplace Dynamics on February 28

  • Amazon (North America / EU / Japan): Post-CNY FBA replenishment is landing and the Buy Box is still contestable on robot vacuums, smart rings and multicookers. Vine and early-review velocity is the ranking lever before the spring ramp; expect base prices to harden as Q2 demand builds.
  • TikTok Shop (US / SEA / UK): Discovery commerce stays hottest for beauty devices and fitness apparel; creator coupons are stackable and short-video conversion outpaces PPC on health and beauty.
  • AliExpress (Global / Choice): Choice-subsidy price anchors keep GaN chargers and earbuds cheap; the value shopper who will not wait for spring promos is buying now.
  • Temu (NA / EU): Factory-direct winter floors (heated gear, small appliances) are the clearance margin story; full-managed supply-chain cost is the only edge and prices are at the floor.
  • Shopee / Lazada (SEA): Late-winter clearance stacks and free-shipping promos are live; air purifiers and small home appliances are the regional heat as spring allergy season approaches.
  • Walmart / Others: Home-comfort and meal-prep durables hold steady price and a forgiving return policy, a quiet place to restock before the Q2 repricing.

3. Hot Sectors Across Marketplaces

  1. Post-CNY restock durables - robot vacuums, multicookers and smart home at Q1 base before spring repricing.
  2. Health and fitness tech - smart rings, recovery devices and sleep tech ride the resolution curve into March.
  3. Late-winter clearance - heaters, electric blankets and winter apparel at the floor, gone by mid-March.
  4. Spring allergy prep - air purifiers and HEPA devices climb as pollen season approaches.
  5. Beauty devices - LED and microcurrent masks stay viral on TikTok Shop.
  6. Transitional footwear and activewear - spring apparel lines open as winter clearance exits.

4. Likely Breakouts - Amazon, TikTok Shop and Shopify

  • Amazon breakouts: robot vacuums and multicookers (post-CNY restock at soft base, fat durable margin) and the smart-ring cluster as the health curve converts to durable buys.
  • TikTok Shop breakouts: LED / microcurrent beauty devices and lightweight fitness apparel, driven by creator velocity and clip-coupon economics.
  • Shopify / DTC breakouts: the recovery-device and smart-ring cluster, where brand trust beats marketplace price, and transitional activewear drops from Gymshark-class stores as spring lines open.

7. Forward Outlook - The Worth-Buying Trajectory

The next 30 to 90 days resolves into a clear sequence. Through mid-March the late-winter clearance exits and the last floor prices on heaters and winter apparel disappear, so sweep those now. From March the spring ramp reprices restock durables and opens transitional apparel and allergy-prep demand; lock Q1-base durables this week before that happens. The health curve stays elevated into Q2, supporting smart-ring and recovery-device demand. Easter (April 21 2029) becomes the next gifting repricing moment, with Mothers Day (May 12) close behind.

The durable conviction: restock spring-adjacent and health durables at the post-CNY base this week, sweep late-winter clearance before mid-March, hold on pure spring loss-leaders until the promotional floor appears, and watch TikTok Shop for the fastest discovery-led breakouts while Amazon and Shopify DTC hold the margin on trusted, brand-led categories.
