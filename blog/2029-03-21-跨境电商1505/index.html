Executive Summary

The cross-border market on 2029-03-21 is a spring-reset board, and the two calendar facts that frame it are both about timing. March 21 is the first full Wednesday after the March 20 spring equinox, so garden and outdoor prep turns from forecast to purchase. It also sits eleven days before Easter Sunday (April 1, 2029) and one week before the AliExpress 3.28 Anniversary Sale, which means the gifting and the 328 warm-up overlap into a single late-March demand spike. The third current is the quiet one: the five-tier US tariff band that went live on 2028-07-01 has now been active for over eight months, so the landed-cost math that dominated 2027 and early 2028 has settled into a known number rather than a moving target.

Three currents define the date, and the order matters.

First, the AliExpress 3.28 Anniversary warm-up is in peak coupon stacking. The 328 pre-heat opened earlier in March, and by March 21 the voucher tiers are deep enough that the anchor stack (platform voucher plus store coupon plus coins plus bank rebate) shows a real saving at checkout. This is the first date a buyer can screenshot an actual stacked total for the spring’s biggest fungible-volume sale.

Second, pre-Easter gifting is eleven days out. Easter Sunday falls on April 1, 2029, which doubles as Q2 start, so the late-March window is the last clean gifting run before the holiday weekend. Skincare, small leather goods, giftable home textiles and spring decor all convert on the Easter-gifting logic, and they convert on distributed-node DTC fulfilment that dodges any parcel dwell.

Third, the five-tier tariff band has gone quiet. The structure that replaced the post-repeal gap on 2028-07-01 is now mature, so buyers importing fungible volume can price landed cost with confidence instead of hedging against a re-band. The EU side is unchanged underneath: the 150 EUR de-minimis abolition (live since 2026-07-01) with a flat 3 EUR per tariff sub-heading, plus the Union handling fee live since November 2026, remain the standing cost lines. Quiet tariff math is good math for peak-season planning.

Heating up on March 21: spring garden and outdoor, beauty and glow serums, 3.28 warm-up fungibles (home organization, tableware, pet cooling), spring-apparel transition pieces, and giftable small leather goods and skincare for the Easter and early Mother’s Day run. Pre-Easter gifting runs through March 31, then hands off to the early Mother’s Day build that opens in April. Beauty, glow, small-batch kitchen and giftable leather goods are the four sectors that compound through the warm-up into the spring gifting run.

Spring garden and outdoor have the longest runway. The durable edge remains fulfilment location. Stores and SKUs that ship from in-region or distributed nodes will keep clearing while single-origin CN post absorbs any seasonal dwell. With the five-tier band now quiet, the structural read for the rest of Q2 is location, not tariff math.
