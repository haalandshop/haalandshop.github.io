Sector Rotation: What Is Heating Up and Cooling Down

Heating up on January 30: Valentine’s gifting and self-care, small air-friendly beauty and jewelry, CN-origin fungibles already warehoused in-region, 2.2 pre-add electronics and home-entertainment, Super Bowl game-day small appliances and party decor, and winter home comfort that survives the season transition. Skip generic trend apparel with no season-transition or Valentine’s logic; the Cabled Knit Cardigan works because it bridges winter to spring, but a plain summer tee does not.

Three risks sit under every buy on January 30.
