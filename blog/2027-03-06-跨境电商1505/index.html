Independent Shopify DTC stores rotated standalone acquisition offers into bundles at the month turn, settled them through March 2, and have not moved since. On March 6 that settled state is deep inside the weekend freeze, and because these stores reprice manually rather than algorithmically, the freeze is absolute - no engine adjusts a sticker overnight, and no human does either on a Saturday. Effective first-order cost sits stable above the February 28 low but well below the confirmed marketplace equivalents, and it will hold through Sunday. The clearest DTC price of the freeze week is now, and a buyer who wants to think it over has a genuine 48 hours to do so at an identical number.

Extended return windows persist on the largest stores

The new DTC signal that began appearing Friday continues through the weekend: the largest independent stores have extended return windows ahead of the spring deal events and Easter on March 28. Because the signal is per-store policy rather than a cohort-wide mechanic, the work is local - checking the actual policy page per store. Regional stock keeps the edge as the lanes normalize

The DTC delivery advantage was at its most concrete during the peak and narrows slightly as the surge clears, but it does not disappear. A regionally stocked DTC order still means two-to-five-day delivery, no cross-border latency and a flat shipped price, against a marketplace lane that is normalizing rather than fast. For known-specification self-use items, the DTC sweet spot holds: launch-priced goods at near-staple speed on a frozen price, with the option to decide over the weekend without penalty. The congestion was the urgent reason to prefer DTC this week; the structural reasons - flat pricing, no repricing engine, regional speed - are why the preference persists after it clears.

The de minimis and compliance floor

The United States remains without the 800 dollar de minimis lane, switched off for every country on August 29 2025, and the repricing work is largely absorbed. The UK 135 GBP threshold remains in consultation. None of this moves today’s decisions, but it is the floor these settled launch prices rest on, and it is why tariff-exposed relaunches carry more drift risk than domestic-stock DTC does.
