Five-plus weeks into the post-Mother’s-Day (May 9) reset and the final week of June, Amazon US and EU storefronts remain dominated by dependable, compliance-heavy categories: Home & Kitchen, Beauty & Personal Care, Consumer Electronics, Pet Supplies, and Health & Wellness. Steam mops (Shark posted a 5,800% order increase March-May), cordless blenders (Ninja Blast’s 1,800% order jump), and collectible toys (Labubu, 1.1B views, POP MART up 819% orders in the same window) are the breakout archetypes. New rising micro-trends — Barrier Moisturizer (+4,500% in 30 days) and B12 Gummies (+2,800%) — signal the next wave of skin-barrier and wellness-content breakouts. The platform rewards micro-creators (10K-100K followers) over mega-influencers for niche products, and live shopping is now a co-equal discovery surface in North America and Southeast Asia. On 2027-06-28, the Monday creator cadence resets after the weekend peak as live-shopping inventory refills with core variants and the July 4 holiday angle pushes portable cooling, travel tech, and patriotic party/decor content; the Labubu blind boxes and peel-off lip stains hold their remaining hero core while the grid refills, and the content-commerce engine pivots fully from weekend impulse and Father’s Day gifting (now closed for cross-border) to self-purchase, summer-prep restock, and pre-July-4 party buys ahead of the mid-July Prime Day window; the early-June price is already drifting up under the June repricing, and the pre-July-4 outdoor-and-travel angle keeps portable cooling, grilling accessories, and travel organizers in the spotlight.

Shopify-native stores win on products where brand trust, subscription cadence, and customer data matter more than price comparison. On June 28, DTC brands hold the deepest full-selection stock and the margin advantage normalizes slightly as the marketplace grid refills from its Monday June 28 trough at the month’s highest reprice floor, keeping summer-refresh, travel, and home-refresh bundles with free-shipping thresholds calibrated to the weekday order flow, with the June peak-season repricing holding full swing and keeping the marketplace-DTC margin gap meaningful through the trough. 2.4 Temu, Shopee & AliExpress — the value lane

Temu’s full-managed model keeps margins razor-thin and rewards factory-direct supply chains; it has shifted toward localized overseas inventory as the EU import-duty regime on low-value parcels (the end of the de-minimis exemption) is now fully in force, raising the value-lane’s landed cost on budget parcels into Europe. Shopee remains the Southeast Asia leader (paired with TikTok Shop), and AliExpress holds strength in Europe, the Middle East, and Latin America, though EU duty on sub-threshold goods is pushing both toward local warehousing. These channels are best for price-sensitive, standardized, high-velocity goods rather than brand-building, and on Monday the value lane runs at steady trough volume as budget shoppers compare the now thin marketplace grid against the lowest price points, with the June repricing holding full swing on marketplace floors pushing marginal traffic onto the value lane during the weekly low of browse.
